### 🏛️ The Ind. Ecosystem Architecture

We are actively engineering a tracking-free, privacy-hardened alternative to foreign proprietary platforms:

* 🗣️ **Communication & Social:** Privacy-first, multilingual interaction networks via **Ind.Samvad** ([samvad.chat](https://samvad.chat)), **Ind.Social** ([ind.social](https://ind.social)), and **Ind.Network** ([ind.network](https://ind.network)).
* 🛡️ **Core Infrastructure & Sovereign Utilities:** Privacy-by-design foundational services including **Ind.Browser**, **Ind.Mail**, and community networks via [ind.center](https://ind.center).
* 📊 **Domain Verticals:**
  * **EdTech & Discovery:** [ind.shiksha](https://ind.shiksha) & [ind.quest](https://ind.quest)
  * **Finance, Trading & Analytics:** [ind.trading](https://ind.trading) & [ind.report](https://ind.report)
  * **Lifestyle & Services:** [ind.run](https://ind.run) & [ind.pet](https://ind.pet)
* 🖥️ **Ind.OS (Long-term Milestone):** A sovereign, privacy-first operating system designed as a secure bridge between hardware and end-users.
* 📦 **Productivity & Utilities:** Unified alternatives across **Ind.Docs**, **Ind.Pay**, **Ind.Health**, and **Ind.Maps**.
